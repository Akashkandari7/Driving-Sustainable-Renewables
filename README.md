# DSR — Driving Sustainable Renewables

**Independent quality, engineering and advisory solutions for solar and energy storage.**

DSR is an independent technical services firm supporting developers, manufacturers and investors across
the solar and battery energy storage value chain — from the factory floor to the grid.

_Independent. Technical. Data-driven._

## Services

**Quality Assurance** — Confidence starts with knowing what you're buying.
Independent verification of product quality and manufacturing processes: factory inspections, quality
audits and supplier assessments across PV modules, cells and balance-of-plant.

**Battery Energy Storage** — Storage you can bank on.
The same independent rigour applied to battery energy storage systems: cell and module inspections,
BESS factory acceptance, and compliance and safety review.

**Engineering & Advisory** — Better technical decisions. Lower project risk.
Technical specification reviews, supplier evaluation, technical due diligence, and procurement and
construction support.

**Digital Intelligence** — Turning quality data into intelligence.
Quality dashboards, AI-enabled analysis and industry benchmarking built on real inspection data.
AI doesn't replace engineering judgement — it strengthens it.
